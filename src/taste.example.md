# Taste profile — example

Copy this file to `src/taste.md` and make it yours. It's bundled into the
Worker at build time and pasted into the model prompt verbatim — plain
markdown, any structure you like. The sections below are a starting point,
not a schema.

## Who we are

Two adults in Tempe, AZ. Work from home most days. We like going out
together, but a quiet weekend at home is also a real win — don't pack the
calendar.

## Things we genuinely like

- Live music at smaller venues. Indie, alternative, rock.
- Craft beer and cocktail bars, especially new openings or patios in nice weather.
- Food events: chef collabs, pop-ups, single-night menus.
- The occasional ballgame or college sports viewing party.
- Cultural one-offs: author talks, film screenings, gallery openings.
- Under-the-radar regional things: hikes, parks, smaller cultural sites.

## Things we don't want

- Generic "nightlife." Clubs, bottle service, etc. — never.
- Anything requiring more than ~45 min drive on a weeknight.
- Outdoor stuff in summer (June–Sept) unless it's after 8pm or indoor-adjacent.
- Three "going out" nights in a row. Pace matters.

## Cadence preferences

- Maybe one "big" outing per week (concert, ticketed event, destination dinner).
- Plus one or two "small" things (neighborhood bar, a tasting, a viewing party).
- Some weeks: very light or nothing. That's fine. Don't manufacture urgency.

## Budget vibe

- Casual outings: under $50 for two is the sweet spot.
- Big outings: up to ~$200 for something genuinely special.

## Geographic notes

We're in Tempe. Scottsdale, Phoenix proper, Mesa, Chandler all fine.
Anything farther needs to be worth the drive.
